# Diet Chart Generation Rules & Admin Panel Display Reference

This rule defines the complete set of business, safety, scoring, and nutritional rules that govern the automated diet chart generation engine in **Nutribuddy**, as well as how they are displayed and managed in the Admin Panel.

---

## 1. Admin Panel Display Architecture

- **Route:** `admin.nutrition.diet-rules.index` (`GET /admin/nutrition/diet-rules`)
- **Controller:** [DietRuleController](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/app/Http/Controllers/Admin/Nutrition/DietRuleController.php)
- **Blade View:** [resources/views/admin/nutrition/rules/index.blade.php](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/resources/views/admin/nutrition/rules/index.blade.php)
- **Navigation Location:** Admin Sidebar > **Diet Chart** > **Diet Generation Rules**
- **Permissions:** Restricted under Spatie permission `view diet chart` or `manage diet chart`.

## 2. Diet Chart Generation Rules Matrix

All automated diet plans generated by [RuleBasedDietPlanEngine](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/app/Services/Nutrition/RuleBasedDietPlanEngine.php) adhere strictly to the following 5 rule categories:

### A. Safety & Dietary Filtering Rules ([FoodFilterPipeline](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/app/Services/Nutrition/FoodFilterPipeline.php))
*These are hard filters. Any food failing these criteria is eliminated prior to scoring.*

1. **Dietary Preference:**
   - **Vegan:** Requires `is_vegan = 1`. Hard excludes any food with `contains_dairy = 1`, `contains_egg = 1`, or `contains_honey = 1`.
   - **Jain:** Requires `is_jain_ok = 1` (excludes root vegetables, onion, garlic, etc.).
   - **Eggetarian:** Excludes red meat, poultry, and fish, while permitting egg items.
   - **Vegetarian:** Requires `is_veg = 1`. Excludes meat, poultry, fish, and eggs (`contains_egg = 0`).
   - **Non-Vegetarian:** Permits all eligible food items.
2. **Allergen Exclusions:**
   - **Dairy:** Excludes foods where `contains_dairy = 1`.
   - **Nuts:** Excludes foods where `contains_nuts = 1`.
   - **Gluten:** Excludes foods where `contains_gluten = 1`.
   - **Egg:** Excludes foods where `contains_egg = 1`.
3. **Disliked Food Exclusion:**
   - Hard excludes any food whose dish name matches the user's questionnaire dislikes.

---

### B. Slot & Timing Rules
*Determines the daily structure and meals.*

1. **Meal Slot Matching:**
   - Foods must be tagged for the corresponding slot via `slot` or `also_suitable_for` (e.g., Breakfast, Morning Snack, Lunch, Evening Snack, Dinner).
2. **Meal Patterns:**
   - **"3 meals":** Generates Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner.
   - **"2 + snacks":** Generates Breakfast, Morning Snack, Lunch, and Evening Snack.
   - **Default (5 occasions):** Generates Breakfast, Morning Snack, Lunch, Evening Snack, and Dinner.
3. **Weekly Schedule:**
   - Generates a full 7-day schedule (Monday through Sunday) for all designated slots.

---

### C. Nutritional Targeting & Gap Filling Rules ([TargetEngine](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/app/Services/Nutrition/TargetEngine.php))

1. **ICMR Reference Target:**
   - Fetches Recommended Dietary Allowances (RDA) from `icmr_nutrition_targets` based on the child's exact age and gender.
2. **Daily Nutrient Tracking:**
   - Tracks 17 key nutrients across each day: Energy (kcal), Protein (g), Fat (g), Fiber (g), Calcium (mg), Iron (mg), Zinc (mg), Magnesium (mg), Vitamin A (mcg), Vitamin C (mg), Vitamin D (mcg), Thiamin B1 (mg), Riboflavin B2 (mg), Niacin B3 (mg), Vitamin B6 (mg), Folate (mcg), and Vitamin B12 (mcg).
3. **Gap-Filling Scoring Bonus:**
   - Foods providing nutrients that fulfill remaining daily requirements receive a proportional score boost up to `+30`.
4. **Over-Nutrition Guard:**
   - Prevents excessive concentration of daily micronutrients in a single meal.
5. **Dairy Consumption Cap:**
   - If the child profile indicates consumption of $\ge 2$ glasses of milk/dairy daily, subsequent dairy items receive a `-4` score penalty to prevent excessive calcium from inhibiting iron absorption.

---

### D. Variety, Regional & Preference Rules

1. **Repetition Penalty:**
   - Foods have a configurable repeat gap (`repeat_gap_days`, default: 3 days). If a food was served within that window, it receives a `-20` penalty. The engine prioritizes fresh items, only relaxing variety if candidate food counts are depleted.
2. **Regional Affinity Bonus:**
   - Foods matching the family's preferred culinary region (e.g., North Indian, South Indian) receive a `+5` score boost.
3. **Kid Acceptance Rating:**
   - Foods rated with higher child acceptance (`kid_accept_1_5`) receive up to `+5` bonus points.
4. **Cooking Medium Compatibility:**
   - Dishes compatible with the family's preferred cooking oil/fat receive a `+5` score boost.
5. **Probabilistic Weighted Selection:**
   - The engine selects probabilistically across the top 5 candidates based on their weighted scores to ensure organic variation.

---

### E. Clinical Risk & Deficiency Rules ([RiskFlaggingEngine](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/app/Services/Nutrition/RiskFlaggingEngine.php))

1. **Clinical Symptom Correlation:**
   - Correlates questionnaire visible signs (e.g., pale conjunctiva, brittle nails, fatigue) with [DeficiencyRule](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/app/Models/Nutrition/DeficiencyRule.php) records to trigger clinical gap flags.
2. **Professional Review Gate:**
   - Any medical condition recorded in the assessment sets `professional_review_required = true`, placing the diet chart in `pending_review` status for certified nutritionist validation.

## 3. Maintenance Guidelines

- **Updating Rule Descriptions in UI:** When modifying diet chart scoring or filtering logic in [RuleBasedDietPlanEngine](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/app/Services/Nutrition/RuleBasedDietPlanEngine.php), always update the corresponding card in [resources/views/admin/nutrition/rules/index.blade.php](file:///e:/website-project/Nutribuddy/resources/views/admin/nutrition/rules/index.blade.php) to maintain transparency for dietitians and admins.
- **Null Safety in Scoring:** Always ensure fallback default values when reading nutrient values from food records (`$food->nutrients->{$nutrient} ?? 0`).
