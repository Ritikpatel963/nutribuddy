Common Questions
Parents Ask, We Answer
Is this plan suitable for vegetarian / Jain families?
Absolutely. During profile setup, you can specify vegetarian, vegan, Jain (no root vegetables), or any other dietary restriction. Every recommendation will respect your preferences without compromising nutritional completeness.
How is this different from a generic diet chart from Google?
Generic charts don't know your child's age, weight, zone, food preferences, seasonal availability, or existing deficiencies. KidzNourish combines all of these factors in real-time, uses WHO and IAP paediatric guidelines, and adjusts as your child grows. It's the difference between a template and a tailor-made plan.
Can I use this for more than one child?
Yes! You can add multiple child profiles under one parent account. Each child gets their own personalised plan based on their age, growth data, and preferences. Multi-child discounts are available at checkout.
What if my child has a food allergy?
Allergies and intolerances are collected during profile setup. Our system strictly excludes any allergen and suggests nutritionally equivalent alternatives — so a child allergic to dairy gets their calcium from ragi, sesame, and fortified foods instead.
How often does the plan change?
Weekly subscribers get a fresh plan every 7 days. Monthly and quarterly subscribers also get automatic seasonal updates (summer, monsoon, winter) and milestone-based updates whenever you log a growth change. No two months are ever identical.
Do I need to buy special or expensive ingredients?
Never. Our Zone-Smart™ algorithm is designed around ingredients that are easily available in your local market or kirana store. We don't recommend superfoods or imports — just real, affordable, Indian food done right.
Is my child's data safe and private?
Yes. We are fully DPDP Act (India) compliant. Child health data is encrypted at rest and in transit, never sold to third parties, and can be deleted at any time on request. We take children's privacy extremely seriously.
